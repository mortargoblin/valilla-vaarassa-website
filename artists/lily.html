Re-imagining Francisco Miranda’s visit to Imatra Falls

Lily Díaz-Kommonen

Using a hybrid approach including documentary, the installation brings together a lenticular print of the Siitola Manor (Siitolan kartano), the place where Francisco Miranda spent the night during his visit to Imatra Falls 23 August 1787 with a Moriche hammock created by native inhabitants from the Orinoco River in Venezuela. Descriptions of Imatra and its inhabitants – written by Miranda in his diary – will be read throughout the exhibition. The diary is part of UNESCO Memory of the World programme, (http://franciscodemiranda.org/inicio).

Dokumentaarinen hybridi-installaatio yhdistää lentikulaarisen printin Siitolan kartanosta, jossa Francisco Miranda yöpyi matkallaan Imatrankoskelle 23.8.1787, sekä venezuelalaisen Orinoco-joen alkuperäiskansan Moriche-riippumaton. Näyttely toisintaa Mirandan päiväkirjan kuvauksia Imatrasta ja sen asukkaista. Päiväkirja on osa UNESCOn maailmanperintöohjelmaa. (http://franciscodemiranda.org/inicio).
